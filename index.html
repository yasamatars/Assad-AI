FAQ

Questions, answered

What does the AI voice agent sound like? +

Natural and conversational, trained on your business's tone and common questions. Most callers don't realize they're talking to an AI until you tell them.

What happens if the agent can't handle a question? +

It hands the call off to your team with full context — no dead air, no lost lead, and a note in the CRM so nothing falls through.

What shows up in my CRM dashboard? +

Every call, transcript, and outcome — organized into the pipeline stages your team already uses, with live reporting on the numbers you care about.

How long does a typical build take? +

Most engagements run 1–2 weeks from kickoff to launch, depending on how many systems we're connecting and how custom the dashboard needs to be.

How much does a project cost? +

It depends on scope. Book a free strategy call and we'll give you a fixed quote after understanding your call volume and workflow — no surprise hours billed.
